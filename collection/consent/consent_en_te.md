# Voice recording consent (read and agree before calling)

**Project:** Telugu voice research by telugu-voice-lab (contact: <your email / WhatsApp>)

**What we record:** Your voice reading about 50 short sentences over a normal phone call (about 15 minutes).
Some sentences contain made-up names, phone numbers and vehicle numbers. Please read them as written; do not use your own.

**Why:** To test how well speech recognition systems understand Telugu and Telugu-English on real phone lines.

**What we store:** The audio, a speaker code (not your name), region, approximate age band, gender, phone carrier and
recording setting. Your phone number is stored only as a one-way hash. We do not store your address.

**Who can access it:** The project team, and speech-API providers that process the audio for testing
(currently Sarvam AI and Deepgram), under their terms.

**Your rights:** Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time before or after recording. Message us with your
speaker code and we will delete your recordings within 7 days. You may skip any sentence.

**Payment:** Rs____ via UPI after the session, whether or not you complete all sentences.
