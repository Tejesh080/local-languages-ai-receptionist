# Customer discovery: 20 businesses, in parallel with E1-E7

Mix: 10 automotive service (5 two-wheeler, 5 car multi-brand or authorised), 5 diagnostic centres, 5 clinics.
Cities: Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Tirupati, Warangal. Script: docs/research/28-next-experiments.md (listen first, no pitch).

**Counts as demand:** yes to a free 30-day pilot; agreement to forward missed / after-hours calls; introduction to the
decision maker; willingness to share workflow data (service list, prices, slot rules); a stated monthly price they would pay.
**Does not count:** "sounds interesting", "send details", compliments.

Record every interview in `interview_tracker.csv` the same day. Automotive is the BENCHMARK domain; the commercial
vertical is decided by this tracker, not by the benchmark.
